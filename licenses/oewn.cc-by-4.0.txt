Open English WordNet
====================

The offline dictionary and thesaurus data bundled with WriteRight (the compact
binaries in public/lexicon/) is derived from Open English WordNet 2025+.

  "Open English WordNet" by the Open English WordNet contributors
  https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet
  https://en-word.net/

  Derived in part from Princeton WordNet 3.0
  Copyright 2006 by Princeton University

Open English WordNet is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International License (CC-BY-4.0). To view a copy of this license, visit:

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

WriteRight extracts and redistributes only the subset of this data required for
its "define" and "synonyms" features (lemma, part of speech, definition, one
example sentence, synset co-members, and direct antonyms), transformed into a
compact block-compressed binary. This constitutes an adapted work under the
license; attribution is preserved here and in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
